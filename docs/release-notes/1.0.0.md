### First release

This is a fork of [DynamicNotch](https://github.com/jackson-storm/DynamicNotch) 1.6.5. It includes the original's changes through 2026-11-24. These are new lock screen media controls, simpler lock screen and Face ID settings, and gesture settings on the Notch page.

- On the first launch, the app draws "hello" in the system accent color.
- The menu bar icon shows the face from the app icon.
- The island morphs from one live activity to the next instead of emptying in between.
- Pressing a tab or a button near the top of a window no longer starts a window drag.
- The Focus monitor no longer leaves a `log stream` process running after the app is killed.
- The app reduces repeated work in 20 places.
- The app ships in English only.
- The Downloads live activity is removed.
- The Calendar settings have a switch that leaves all-day events out of the notch.
- Updates come from this repository.
